localinfine@gmail.com

Common questions

The voice (TTS) isn't working

Citolex uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voices. Make sure your device's volume is up and the app isn't muted by a hardware side switch. If a specific voice sounds wrong or is missing, more voices can be downloaded in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.

Adding text

You can paste text directly, upload a file (.txt, .md, .pdf, .docx, or .epub), paste a link to an article, or share text into Citolex from Safari, Notes, or any other app using the share sheet.

Reporting a bug

Please email a short description of what happened, what you expected instead, and your device model and iOS version if you can. Screenshots or screen recordings help a lot.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for details on how Citolex handles your data (short version: everything stays on your device).
